# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Participate with technical honesty, respect, patience, and attention to the
safety of other people and their data. Constructive disagreement about models
and evidence is welcome; harassment, personal attacks, discrimination,
threats, and deliberate disclosure of private information are not.

## Project spaces

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and
other spaces where someone represents this project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact route
on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed in context and
handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
close interactions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
needed to protect the project community.
